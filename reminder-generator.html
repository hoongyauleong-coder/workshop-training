Compliance Reminder Generator

Paste your Excel tracker. Every person gets one reminder covering all their open items, written at the right escalation level, ready to send through ECOM.

1. Paste dari Excel

Select your rows in Excel including the header row, copy, and paste below. Columns recognised (English or BM): Name/Nama, Email/Emel, Department/Jabatan, CAR No, Description/Isu, Due Date/Tarikh Akhir, Status, Manager/Pengurus, Manager Email. Only Name, Due Date and either CAR No or Description are required. Dates are read as dd/mm/yyyy.

Settings: tarikh, escalation levels & signature

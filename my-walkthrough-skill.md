/my-walkthrough — Walkthrough Findings Write-Up for HoongYau's Compliance Work

WHAT IT DOES:
Turns my rough walkthrough notes into formal, numbered findings ready for the monthly inspection report and for issuing CARs.

THE STEPS:
1. Ask me for: date, area/department walked, person in charge of the area, and my rough notes (typed, voice-to-text, or point form — messy is fine).
2. For each issue in my notes, classify it:
   - Category: Environmental (DOE) / Safety & Health (DOSH) / Company SOP
   - Severity: Major NC / Minor NC / Observation
   - Reference: the relevant regulation, ISO 14001 clause, or SOP number
3. Write each finding in this format:
   Finding No. | Area | Description (what I saw, factual) | Requirement (what it should be) | Reference | Severity | Corrective Action Required | PIC | Due Date
4. End with a short summary: total findings by severity and category, plus the top 3 priorities.
5. Deliver it as a table I can paste straight into Excel, followed by a 3-line summary I can paste into ECOM.

RULES:
- Always firm and formal. Mixed BM and English — headings and corrective actions in BM, legal/technical references in English.
- Descriptions must be factual and objective: what was seen, where, when. No opinions, no blame, no names in the description.
- Never invent a regulation number or clause. If unsure of the exact reference, write [SAHKAN RUJUKAN] so I check it.
- Default due dates: Major NC = 7 days, Minor NC = 14 days, Observation = 30 days, unless I say otherwise.
- Output must be copy-paste ready. No extra commentary before or after the table.
